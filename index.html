The Composition

Five Notes, One Signature

Every Noir & Ambre bottle is built from the same five-note skeleton, reweighted for each fragrance in the line.

  • Bergamot — a bright, citrus opening that fades within minutes.
  • Jasmine Sambac — hand-picked at night, when the petals are most fragrant.
  • Oud Wood — smoked and aged, the dark spine of every blend.
  • Amber Resin — warms the composition and helps it last on skin.
  • White Musk — the final, almost-silent note that lingers longest.

How To Wear It

The Application Ritual

  1. 01 Warm the nozzle in your palm for a few seconds before the first spray.
  2. 02 Apply to pulse points — wrists, the base of the throat, behind each ear.
  3. 03 Never rub your wrists together; it breaks down the top notes early.
  4. 04 Let the fragrance dry naturally for sixty seconds before dressing.
  5. 05 Re-apply lightly after eight hours rather than over-spraying at once.
